# Google Play screenshots — English (United States)

Status: **improved v2 draft pending visual approval**.

The recommended v2 compositions are stored in `final-v2/`. They are opaque
24-bit PNG files measuring 1080 × 1920 pixels. The first localized set remains
in `final/` only as a comparison and should not be uploaded if v2 is approved.

## Upload order and alt text

1. `01_widget_en_v2.png`
   - Heading: `Tap the widget and speak. No need to open the app.`
   - Alt text: Nolvida home screen widget recording a voice reminder, with a
     stop control and voice waveform.
2. `02_notifications_en_v2.png`
   - Heading: `Your reminders stay visible until you're done`.
   - Alt text: Grouped Nolvida notification keeping three pending reminders
     visible on the phone.
3. `03_capture_en_v2.png`
   - Heading: `Capture reminders by voice or text`.
   - Alt text: Empty Nolvida home screen with options to create a note or capture
     a reminder by voice.
4. `04_reminders_en_v2.png`
   - Heading: `Your reminders, at a glance`.
   - Alt text: Nolvida home screen with three reminders and controls to complete
     them, create a new note, or use voice capture.

These compositions are localized adaptations of the approved Spanish assets.
The widget and notification are larger in v2, the headings are shorter and the
example meeting uses `Max` instead of `Adrian`. The images must not introduce
controls, features, awards, prices, ratings, or calls to download the app.
