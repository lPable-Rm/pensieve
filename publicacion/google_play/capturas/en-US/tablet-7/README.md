# Google Play screenshots — 7-inch tablet — English (United States)

Status: **final candidates pending visual approval**.

The source screenshots supplied from the Android tablet emulator remain in
`raw/`. The four upload-ready compositions are in `final/`, as opaque 24-bit
PNG files measuring 1080 × 1920 pixels.

## Upload order and alt text

1. `01_widget_tablet7_en.png`
   - Heading: `Tap the widget and speak. No need to open the app.`
   - Alt text: Large Nolvida tablet widget ready to capture a voice reminder
     without opening the app.
2. `02_notifications_tablet7_en_v2.png`
   - Heading: `Your reminders stay visible until you're done`.
   - Alt text: Grouped Nolvida notification keeping three pending reminders
     visible on a tablet.
3. `03_capture_tablet7_en.png`
   - Heading: `Capture reminders by voice or text`.
   - Alt text: Nolvida empty state on a tablet with voice and manual reminder
     controls.
4. `04_reminders_tablet7_en_v2.png`
   - Heading: `Your reminders, at a glance`.
   - Alt text: Nolvida tablet screen with three reminder cards and controls to
     complete them, create a note, or use voice capture.

The compositions are based on the real 7-inch tablet states in `raw/`. The
cropped low-resolution references were rebuilt for legibility and their three
example notes were localized as `change the padel match time`, `meeting at
5:00 PM`, and `buy coffee capsules`. No unimplemented functionality is
represented.

The files `02_notifications_tablet7_en.png` and
`04_reminders_tablet7_en.png` are the previous comparison variants and must not
be uploaded when using v2.
