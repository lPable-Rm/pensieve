# Google Play screenshots — 10-inch tablet — English (United States)

Status: **upload-ready**.

The four assets in `final/` are deterministic high-resolution adaptations of
the approved 7-inch tablet set. They are opaque 24-bit PNG files measuring
2160 × 3840 pixels, with a 9:16 portrait aspect ratio.

## Upload order and alt text

1. `01_widget_tablet10_en.png`
   - Alt text: Large Nolvida tablet widget ready to capture a voice reminder
     without opening the app.
2. `02_notifications_tablet10_en.png`
   - Alt text: Grouped Nolvida notification keeping three pending reminders
     visible on a tablet.
3. `03_capture_tablet10_en.png`
   - Alt text: Nolvida empty state on a tablet with voice and manual reminder
     controls.
4. `04_reminders_tablet10_en.png`
   - Alt text: Nolvida tablet screen with three reminder cards and controls to
     complete them, create a note, or use voice capture.

The visual content, wording and composition are unchanged from the approved
7-inch set. Only the pixel dimensions were increased for the 10-inch Play Store
section.
